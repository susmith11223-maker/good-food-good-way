The 6 rules of better food

Small changes can make a surprisingly big difference.

🧂

Season in layers

Add small amounts of salt and seasoning while cooking instead of trying to fix everything at the end.

🔥

Use the right heat

High heat can brown food beautifully, while lower heat helps delicate foods cook evenly without burning.

🍋

Add brightness

A squeeze of lemon or a little vinegar can wake up rich or heavy food and make flavors pop.

🥓

Mix textures

Combine something crispy with something soft, creamy with crunchy, or juicy with a toasted element.

🌿

Finish fresh

Fresh herbs, green onions, citrus zest, or a little sauce added at the end can make food taste fresher.

👃

Use your senses

Look, smell, taste, and listen. Good cooking is not just following a timer—it is noticing what the food needs.

Quick upgrades you can try today

Before you add another ingredient, try improving what you already have.

🍝 Pasta: Save a little pasta water and use it to make the sauce smoother.
🍚 Rice: Add aromatics like garlic or ginger while cooking for more flavor.
🍕 Pizza: Finish with fresh basil, chili flakes, or a small drizzle of olive oil.
🥪 Sandwiches: Toast the bread and add a crunchy ingredient for contrast.
🥔 Potatoes: Crisp the outside and season while they are hot.
🍳 Eggs: Cook gently and finish with pepper, herbs, or a little cheese.

Make it better

Pick a food and use the upgrade path.

✨ Better instant noodles

  • Use a little less seasoning packet if it is too salty.
  • Add an egg or vegetables for more substance.
  • Finish with green onion, sesame, or chili oil.

🍓 Better yogurt bowl

  • Start with thick yogurt.
  • Add fruit plus something crunchy like granola or nuts.
  • Finish with cinnamon or a small drizzle of honey.

🧀 Better grilled cheese

  • Butter the bread evenly.
  • Use cheese that melts well.
  • Cook over medium-low heat so the bread browns before it burns.

🍌 Better banana toast

  • Toast the bread until crisp.
  • Add banana slices and a thin layer of peanut or seed butter.
  • Finish with cinnamon.
